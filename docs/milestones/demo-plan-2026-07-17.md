## 2. Stay recommendations

### s1: overnight after the late-night 7.17 landing (transiting Hong Kong early next morning)
- **Economy** Yass International Hotel (Shenzhen Airport T3 branch) — about ¥218: free airport shuttle; but the next day you must return to the city center before crossing the border
- **Convenience** chain hotel by Futian Port — about ¥320: about 1h and ¥120 by taxi after landing; cross the border on foot at 06:30, buying time for the 09:00 permit anchor
- **Comfort** Hyatt House at Shenzhen Airport (inside the terminal) — about ¥450: sleep the moment you land; but the next morning requires an early border crossing
- **Recommended**: convenience tier (by the port): the a2 permit anchor opens at 09:00; staying by the port is the only option that lets you sleep to 6:40 and still be unhurried (full-cost view: the door-to-door landing night has already drained your energy, so one more hour of sleep next morning is the highest-value spend)

### s2: Phuket workation, two weeks (13 nights, incl. 10 working days) + girlfriend's dive trips
- **Economy** Kata/Karon apartment-style (self-catering) — about ¥1820 (140/night): $18-25/night; 20-30min drive from Chalong Pier
- **Comfort** Chalong/Rawai serviced apartments (with coworking) — about ¥3900 (300/night): HOMA-type; workstation standard: WiFi + work area + within 10min of the dive pier; 20-30% negotiable for a two-week stay
- **Premium** beachfront resort apartment (with desk) — about ¥7150 (550/night): work experience and resort experience in one
- Location: Chalong/Rawai base (digital-nomad zone + dive pier, a double match); the 『万xx』 candidates (Khao Lak / Krabi Ao Nang / Trang) are held as options for a 2-3-night mid-trip relocation, pending a gate verdict

### s3: Bangkok weekend (2 nights)
- **Economy** three-star along the BTS (Mo Chit/Sukhumvit) — about ¥480 (240/night)
- **Comfort** four-star Sukhumvit/Siam (direct BTS access) — about ¥1056 (528/night)

### s4: Yunnan week (6 nights, pure travel on leave; Kunming is the in/out airport; loop route pending a gate)
- **Economy** Dali (大理)/Lijiang (丽江) off-season weekly guesthouse — about ¥1200: off-season monthly rent can go as low as ¥900-1500/month, a full week about ¥200/night
- **Comfort** Erhai (洱海)/old-town view guesthouse — about ¥2400: average ¥408/night, about ¥400/night after negotiating the week

## 4. For you to decide (gates, multiple-choice)

1. **Budget tier**: economy ¥12.6k / comfort ¥16.3k? (the difference mostly buys office quality for the two Phuket weeks)
2. **『万xx』 relocation spot**: research suggestion — **Krabi·Ao Nang** (easy daily life + diving close by, many monsoon-season alternatives for going out to sea) > Khao Lak
   (Similan dive sites closed May-Oct, halving the point of a July trip) > Trang (cheaper and quieter but far). You can also skip the relocation and stay in Chalong all 13 nights.
3. **Timing for Bangkok**: 7.31 Friday evening TG216 (maximizes the weekend; workation wraps at 16:55 that day) / 8.1 Saturday morning VZ303 (one more Phuket night, saves ¥400)?
4. **Yunnan loop**: into Kunming → Dali 2 nights → Lijiang 2 nights → Kunming 1 night (classic) / Dali in depth 4 nights + Kunming 2 nights (slow Erhai stay, closer to this trip's motivation)?

## 5. Data and boundaries (honesty list)

- Schedules are real times from public channels (sources attached in the data pack); **all prices are estimates**; get live quotes before booking;
- July-August is Andaman Sea monsoon season; dive trips are highly weather-dependent; the east-side Phuket dive sites (Racha/Coral Island direction) are steadier;
- Thailand's visa-free policy and the Hong Kong permit paperwork are **pre-check items**, outside the engine's scope;
- Not modeled by the engine: visas, insurance details, the girlfriend's independent itinerary.
